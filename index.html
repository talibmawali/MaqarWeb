About the Studio عن الاستوديو

A practice between disciplines — drawing on inherited geometries, working with quiet materials, and translating both into spaces that can be entered or scrolled.

Atelier Maram is the practice of Layla Khoury — an architect and digital artist working between Beirut, Sharjah, and Berlin.

Founded in 2018, the studio operates at the intersection of built environments and computational art. Our work translates the geometries of the Levant and the Gulf — the muqarnas, the mashrabiya, the courtyard, the alley — into spaces that feel inherited and new at once.

We build slowly. A pavilion may take three years; a digital archive, five. We collaborate with cultural institutions, foundations, and artists whose work asks the same questions about pattern, memory, and place.

مَرام هو الاستوديو الذي تديره ليلى خوري، معماريّة وفنّانة رقميّة تعمل بين بيروت والشارقة وبرلين. تأسس عام ٢٠١٨، ويعمل عند تقاطع البيئة المبنيّة والفنّ الحاسوبيّ، مترجماً هندسة المشرق والخليج إلى فضاءات موروثة ومستحدثة في آنٍ واحد.
